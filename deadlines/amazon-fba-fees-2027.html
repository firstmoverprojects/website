What should Amazon sellers check about the 2027 FBA fee changes?

Amazon's November 5, 2026 letter to selling partners says most 2027 US referral and FBA fee changes take effect on January 15, 2027. On average, FBA fulfillment fees increase by $0.19 per unit sold, approximately 0.8% of the average item's selling price, and the letter says some products go up while others go down. The FBA fuel and logistics surcharge stays the same. Effective February 1, 2027, Amazon will eliminate the storage utilization surcharge and combine the aged-inventory surcharge with monthly storage into one age-based rate. Effective February 1, the low-inventory-level fee applies only when inventory falls below 28 days of supply, based on actual shipments in the four weeks before and the four weeks after the assessment date.

What to check in your account

  1. Open the Fee Change Comparison dashboard in Profit Analytics. The letter says this is where you see the account-level and product-level effect. The $0.19 figure is an average, not a rate for every SKU.
  2. Open the Revenue Calculator for a product you want to model. The letter names that calculator for fee scenarios.
  3. Treat January 15, 2027 as the date for most changes, and February 1, 2027 as the date the letter gives for the storage-fee redesign.
  4. For storage, the letter says the storage utilization surcharge goes away, which Amazon says makes it easier to stock up before high-demand events. The aged-inventory surcharge is removed and combined with monthly storage into one age-based rate. Amazon says fees are flat or lower for inventory that turns in under six months, and higher for aged inventory.
  5. For the low-inventory-level fee, compare the old test with the new one. The letter says today the fee uses trailing 30- and 90-day sales. Effective February 1, it applies only when inventory is below 28 days of supply, based on actual shipments in the four weeks before the assessment date and the four weeks after it. Amazon says that is meant to catch SKUs that need replenishment and to reduce fees triggered by a demand spike or by end-of-season and end-of-life products.
  6. Leave the fuel and logistics surcharge as it is unless Amazon updates the letter. The letter says that surcharge is unchanged, that fuel and line-haul trucking costs have stayed elevated since it was introduced in April 2026, and that Amazon will keep reviewing it.
  7. For the rest, open 2027 US Referral and Fulfillment by Amazon fees. The letter says that page is the full summary, including additional updates for small and large standard-size items, items priced above $50, "Buy X, Get Y" promotions, Multichannel Fulfillment, and Amazon Warehousing and Distribution. This page does not restate those rates.

Source: Amazon Selling Partners letter, November 5, 2026

FAQ

When do the 2027 US FBA fee changes start?

Amazon's November 5, 2026 letter says most changes take effect on January 15, 2027. The same letter says the storage-fee changes are effective February 1, 2027. For the low-inventory-level fee, the letter says the new rule is effective February 1.

By how much are FBA fulfillment fees changing?

The letter says that on average, FBA fulfillment fees will increase by $0.19 per unit sold, approximately 0.8% of the average item's selling price. It also says the impact varies by product, size, and price, and that fees for some products will increase while others will decrease.

What happens to FBA storage fees on February 1, 2027?

The letter says Amazon will eliminate the storage utilization surcharge and will remove the aged inventory surcharge, combining it with monthly storage fees into a single age-based rate. Amazon says that results in flat or lower fees for inventory that turns in under six months, and higher fees for aged inventory.

How does the low-inventory-level fee change?

The letter says the fee is assessed today using trailing 30- and 90-day sales. Effective February 1, it applies only when inventory falls below 28 days of supply, based on actual shipments in both the four weeks before and the four weeks after the assessment date.

Is the FBA fuel and logistics surcharge changing?

No. The letter says the FBA fuel and logistics surcharge remains unchanged. It says costs, especially fuel and line-haul trucking, have stayed elevated since the surcharge was introduced in April 2026, and that Amazon will keep reviewing it.

Copy-paste prompt for any AI agent

I sell with Fulfillment by Amazon in the US. Using only Amazon's November 5, 2026 letter "Update to US Referral and Fulfillment by Amazon fees for 2027," make a checklist I can take into Seller Central:
1. Quote the date most changes take effect, the average fulfillment-fee change, and the sentence that says some products increase and some decrease.
2. Quote what the letter says about the fuel and logistics surcharge.
3. Quote the February 1, 2027 storage changes, including which surcharges go away and what happens to inventory that turns in under six months versus aged inventory.
4. Quote the low-inventory-level fee change, including the 28-day supply test and the before-and-after shipment window.
5. Do not invent a per-size rate, a storage price, or a referral-fee percent. If the letter only names a topic and points to the "2027 US Referral and Fulfillment by Amazon fees" page, say "see that Seller Central page" instead of guessing the number.
6. Do not log in, and do not ask for my seller password.
When you state a fee or a date, quote the letter. If you cannot find the line, say "no fee line found."
