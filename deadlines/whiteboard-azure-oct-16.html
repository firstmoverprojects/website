Oct 16, 2026

Agent prompt: track this deadline — Old Whiteboards still in Azure (Oct 16)

Work or school Whiteboard users with boards still in the "Not migrated" section.

Key dates

  • Sep 25, 2026

    Boards still in Azure became read-only. They can still be viewed and exported until Oct 16.

    Source: Microsoft Support: Migration of Whiteboards from Azure to OneDrive

  • Oct 16, 2026

    Microsoft says remaining Azure-based Whiteboards are permanently deleted, with no recovery.

    Source: Microsoft Support: Migration of Whiteboards from Azure to OneDrive

Plain-English summary

Microsoft says older work or school Whiteboards that are still stored in Azure and not migrated to OneDrive became read-only on Sep 25 and are permanently deleted on Oct 16, 2026, with no recovery. Until then they can still be viewed and exported. Boards already moved to OneDrive are not part of this. Whiteboard itself is not going away for work or school accounts. Check the "Not migrated" section in Whiteboard.

Source

Microsoft Support: Migration of Whiteboards from Azure to OneDrive · Microsoft Support: Retirement of standalone Microsoft Whiteboard apps (Whiteboard in Teams and on the web continues for work or school accounts).

Checked Oct 12, 2026.

Copy-paste prompt for any AI agent

Microsoft's support page says old work or school Microsoft Whiteboards still stored in Azure (not migrated to OneDrive) are permanently deleted on October 16, 2026. They have been read-only since Sep 25 and can be viewed and exported until then. Please help me track this:
1. Make a checklist for me and each person on my team: "Do you have old Whiteboards? Where are they listed (check the 'Not migrated' section)? Do we need them? Exported yes/no? Who exported? Where saved?"
2. Give me a short message I can send my team asking them to check by a date I choose.
3. Remind me to read Microsoft's migration page for the current steps (Settings, then Export image).
4. Set a reminder schedule: now, three days before, and the day before October 16, 2026.
Do not delete or change anything. Do not ask me for passwords and do not log in to any account. Only list what to check and I will decide.
When you state any deadline or date, quote the exact line from the official source that states it. If you cannot find that line, say "no deadline line found" instead of guessing.
