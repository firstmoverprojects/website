Nov 1, 2026

Until when can customers return Amazon.com items bought in November and December 2026?

Amazon's Seller Central announcement says items customers buy on Amazon.com between November 1, 2026 and December 31, 2026 can be returned until January 31, 2027, except Apple brand products, which can be returned until January 15, 2027.

Amazon says this window applies automatically whether you use Fulfillment by Amazon or fulfill the order yourself, that no action is required from you, and that existing return and refund conditions stay the same. Amazon also says this holiday return policy will apply every year.

Who it affects

Sellers of items on Amazon.com. Amazon's announcement says the extended window applies automatically to all of those items, whether the seller uses Fulfillment by Amazon (FBA) or Merchant Fulfilled (FBM).

It is a return-window change, not a new listing rule. Amazon says existing return and refund conditions remain unchanged, so an item that was not returnable does not become returnable because it was bought in November or December.

What to do

  1. Read the announcement in Seller Central. The page above is Amazon's seller announcement. Sign-in may be required.
  2. Plan for returns of November 1 through December 31, 2026 purchases through January 31, 2027. Amazon's sentence uses the purchase dates, and it sets January 31, 2027 as the return-by date. January 31, 2027 is a Sunday. The announcement does not say the date moves to the next business day.
  3. Treat Apple brand products separately. The same sentence says they can be returned until January 15, 2027. January 15, 2027 is a Friday.
  4. Do not rewrite your return conditions from this page. Amazon says existing return and refund conditions remain unchanged, and that no action is required from you.
  5. Keep this separate from Amazon's November 2, 2026 commercial liability insurance change. That is a different rule, with its own page and its own free certificate check.

FAQ

What is the official return-by date for the 2026 Amazon holiday window?

Amazon's Seller Central announcement says items purchased between November 1, 2026 and December 31, 2026 can be returned until January 31, 2027, except Apple brand products, which can be returned until January 15, 2027.

Does the extended window apply to FBA and to seller-fulfilled orders?

Yes. Amazon says the extended window automatically applies to all items you sell on Amazon.com, regardless of whether you use Fulfillment by Amazon (FBA) or Merchant (FBM).

Do sellers have to change a setting before November 1, 2026?

Amazon says no action is required from you, and that existing return and refund conditions remain unchanged. The announcement also says this holiday policy will apply every year.

Does January 31, 2027 move because it is a Sunday?

Amazon's announcement states January 31, 2027. That calendar day is a Sunday. The announcement does not say the return-by date moves to the next business day. Use the date Amazon published unless Amazon updates the announcement.

Is this the same rule as Amazon's November 2, 2026 insurance change?

No. The holiday return window is about when buyers can send items back. The November 2, 2026 change is about commercial liability insurance for sellers in enhanced-safety categories and for sellers based in Mainland China. The insurance page and the free certificate check cover that rule. They do not check return windows.
