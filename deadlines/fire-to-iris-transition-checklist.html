Transition steps from the IRS pages

  1. Confirm who files your Forms 1099 and other information returns: you, a payroll provider, an accountant, or software. If a payroll provider or accountant files for you, this may already be handled. Ask them.
  2. November 9, 2026 was the last day to make changes to an existing IR Application for TCC; those applications are now read-only.
  3. FIRE filing ended Nov 19, 2026 at 3 p.m. ET. The IRS says you can e-file information returns for tax year 2022 and later, and corrections, in IRIS. The November 1, 2026 test-file date has passed.
  4. Complete an IRIS Application for TCC. Publication 5717 says a FIRE TCC cannot be used in the IRIS Taxpayer Portal, and to allow up to 45 calendar days.
  5. For the 2027 filing season, file tax year 2026 information returns in IRIS. Publication 5717 says IRIS is the only intake system beginning tax year 2026. The IRIS page says the free Taxpayer Portal handles up to 100 returns at a time. Software filers use IRIS Application to Application.

FAQ

What should a FIRE to IRIS transition checklist include?

November 9, 2026 was the last day for changes to an existing IR Application for TCC; those applications are now read-only. FIRE filing ended Nov 19, 2026 at 3 p.m. ET. The IRS says IRIS will be the only e-file system for information returns after Jan. 1, 2027. The checklist also includes a separate IRIS Application for TCC. Publication 5717 says to allow up to 45 calendar days for that application.

What was the last day to change an IR Application for TCC?

November 9, 2026 was the last day to make changes to existing IR Applications for TCCs. The FIRE page says existing applicants could update their applications through November 9, 2026, after which those applications became read-only and are retained for historical reference. The IRS is no longer accepting new IR Applications for TCCs.

When is IRIS the only e-file system?

The FIRE page says IRIS will be the only information returns electronic filing system, including current year, prior year, or corrections, after January 1, 2027. Current FIRE users must complete an IRIS Application for TCC to file tax year 2026 information returns during the 2027 filing season.

How long should the checklist allow for the IRIS TCC application?

Publication 5717 says to allow up to 45 calendar days for application processing.
