Oct 13, 2026

Agent prompt: track this deadline — Office 2021 and Windows 11 24H2 Home/Pro (Oct 13)

Anyone still on Office 2021 or Windows 11 version 24H2 Home or Pro.

Key dates

  • Oct 13, 2026

    Support for Office 2021 ends. Microsoft says there is no extension and no extended security updates.

    Source: Microsoft Support: End of support for Office 2021

  • Oct 13, 2026

    Windows 11 version 24H2 Home and Pro reach end of updates.

    Source: Microsoft Learn: Windows 11, version 24H2 release health · Microsoft Learn: Supported versions of Windows client

  • Oct 12, 2027

    Windows 11 version 24H2 Enterprise and Education editions reach end of updates.

    Source: Microsoft Learn: Supported versions of Windows client

  • 10/14/2026 6:59:59 AM

    Time stamp shown on Microsoft's lifecycle product pages. The support pages say Oct 13, so we use Oct 13.

    Source: Office 2021 lifecycle table · Windows 11 Home and Pro lifecycle table

Plain-English summary

Microsoft's support and release-health pages list October 13, 2026 as the date updates end for Office 2021 and for Windows 11 version 24H2 Home and Pro. After that date, Microsoft says it no longer provides technical support, bug fixes or security fixes for those versions. The apps may keep working. Enterprise and Education editions of 24H2 have a later date (Oct 12, 2027), and Microsoft says unmanaged Home/Pro PCs are offered the 25H2 update automatically. Microsoft's lifecycle product pages show a time stamp of 10/14/2026 6:59:59 AM; the support pages say Oct 13, so we use Oct 13.

Copy-paste prompt for any AI agent

I run a small business. Microsoft's support pages say updates end on October 13, 2026 for Office 2021 and for Windows 11 version 24H2 Home and Pro. Please help me track this:
1. Make a checklist table of every computer we use (name, who uses it, Windows version, Office version).
2. Tell me where to find each item. For Windows, it is Settings > System > About. For Office, open any Office app and look under Account.
3. Mark which computers run Windows 11 24H2 Home or Pro, or Office 2021, and which do not.
4. Remind me to read Microsoft's own pages for the current dates and for options, and list the questions to ask my IT person or vendor.
5. Set up two reminders: today, and the morning of October 13, 2026.
Do not buy or install anything and do not change settings. Do not ask me for passwords or license keys, and do not log in to any account. Only list what I should check and I will decide.
