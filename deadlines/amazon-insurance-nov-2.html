What changes in Amazon insurance on Nov 2, 2026?

Amazon's announcement says two changes start November 2, 2026: sellers with products in categories that have enhanced safety listing requirements need at least $1 million commercial liability coverage per occurrence and in total for those products regardless of the $10,000 monthly sales threshold, for new and existing listings, and sellers based in Mainland China must get new policies through Amazon's Insurance Accelerator program.

Plain-English summary

Amazon's commercial liability insurance page states the existing threshold and policy criteria. Amazon's November 2 announcement states the newer enhanced-safety category and Mainland China requirements. Check those Amazon pages and your own Account Health notice for your case.

Can the newer requirement apply below $10,000 in monthly sales?

Yes, for products in categories with enhanced safety listing requirements. Amazon's November 2 announcement says the USD 1 million requirement applies to those products regardless of the USD 10,000 monthly threshold. Check Amazon's category list and your own Account Health notice.

What should I review with an insurance professional?

Use Amazon's published criteria as the source of truth. The checklist below is a reading aid, not insurance advice or a promise of acceptance.

  1. Amazon states its insured-name rule and the single-member LLC trade-name exception.
  2. Amazon states the additional-insured wording.
  3. Amazon states the per-occurrence and aggregate limits and covered liabilities.
  4. Amazon states the maximum deductible and certificate disclosure requirement.
  5. Amazon states which listed-product sales the policy must cover.
  6. Amazon states the sunset-clause rule.
  7. Amazon states its notice requirement.

Copy-paste prompt for any AI agent

Rule summary source: Amazon's November 2 announcement.

I sell products online. Amazon's announcement says that from November 2, 2026, sellers with products in categories with enhanced safety listing requirements (such as children's, cosmetic and ingestible, and lithium battery products) need commercial liability insurance with at least a $1 million limit regardless of sales volume. Please help me track this:
1. Make a checklist: does my product category fall under the requirement (I will check Amazon's own policy page)? Do I have a current policy? What is its limit, its dates and who is named on it? Who is my insurance contact?
2. List questions to ask an insurance agent, e.g. what documents Amazon asks for and how long it takes.
3. Remind me to read Amazon's own help pages for the exact rules and dates.
4. Set a reminder to re-check Amazon's help pages and your Account Health notice. The November 2, 2026 update has been in effect since that date.
Do not buy anything or submit anything. Only list what I should check and I will decide. Do not ask me for passwords and do not log in to any account.
