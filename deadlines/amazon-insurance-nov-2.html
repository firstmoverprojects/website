Plain-English summary

Amazon already requires commercial liability insurance (at least $1 million per occurrence and in total) for sellers whose Amazon.com sales go over $10,000 in any month. Amazon's announcement says two changes start November 2, 2026. First, sellers with products in categories that have enhanced safety listing requirements need that coverage for those products regardless of the $10,000 threshold, for new and existing listings. Amazon names children's products, cosmetic and ingestible products, and lithium battery products, and says the list is not limited to those. Second, sellers based in Mainland China must get new policies through Amazon's Insurance Accelerator program. Check Amazon's own pages for which rules apply to your products.

Source

Amazon Seller Central announcement: New Commercial Liability Insurance Requirements effective November 2, 2026 (posted by Amazon; Seller Central may ask you to log in). Checked Oct 12, 2026.
