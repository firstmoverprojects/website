Does this loan need a Form 1098-VLI?

The IRS Instructions for Form 1098-VLI (Rev. 12/2026) say to report interest of $600 or more you received on a specified passenger vehicle loan (SPVL) in the course of your trade or business. The $600 threshold applies separately to each SPVL. File a separate Form 1098-VLI for each SPVL.

An SPVL is any indebtedness incurred after 2024 for personal use and that is secured by a first lien. The instructions describe original use that begins with the payer of record, final assembly within the United States, a gross vehicle weight rating under 14,000 pounds, and a car, minivan, van, SUV, pickup, or motorcycle.

The instructions cover that interest even if you are not in the business of lending money. For example, if you are a dealer.

Borrower statements due Jan 31, 2027. IRS e-file due Mar 31, 2027.

If you file 10 or more information returns of any type in the year, you must e-file. For filing season 2027, IRS says IRIS is the only intake system.

IRS Publication 1099 lists payee statements as January 31 for most returns, and says the due date is March 31 if filed electronically. Publication 1099 says a TCC is processed within 45 business days.
