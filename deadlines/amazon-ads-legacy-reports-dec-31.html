Agent prompt: track this deadline — Amazon Ads legacy Sponsored Ads reports retire (Dec 31)

Amazon advertisers who use saved or scheduled reports from the older reports pages.

Key dates

  • Dec 31, 2026

    Amazon Ads says it is sunsetting the Sponsored Ads reports and Amazon DSP reports pages by this date. Unified reporting is under Measurement and Reporting > Reporting.

    Source: Amazon Ads: Campaign analysis with unified reporting, now available

Plain-English summary

Amazon Ads says unified reporting is now generally available in Ads Console (Measurement and Reporting > Reporting). Amazon says it is sunsetting the older Sponsored Ads reports page and the Amazon DSP reports page by December 31, 2026. If you or your agency rely on saved or scheduled reports from those older pages, they need to be recreated in unified reporting. Amazon's June 8, 2026 announcement said an automated tool to move existing scheduled reports would be available soon. Your ad data itself is not described as going away. Check Amazon's own page for current steps and dates.

Source

Amazon Ads: Campaign analysis with unified reporting, now available. Checked Oct 13, 2026.

(Some trade sites report an earlier Dec 17 cutoff for creating new reports. We did not find that date on Amazon's public page, so it is not used here.)
