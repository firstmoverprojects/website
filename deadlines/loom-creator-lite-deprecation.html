What should Loom admins check when Creator Lite is discontinued?

Atlassian says the free Creator Lite role is discontinued. Every Creator Lite user is upgraded to a full Creator seat on your workspace's integration date. Atlassian says you are not charged for those seats during the grace period, which runs until your next billing date. There is no single public date for every workspace. Atlassian says your integration date arrives by email. Recordings stay. Upgraded users lose the 5-minute recording limit and the 25-video limit.

What to check before the integration date

  1. Watch for Atlassian's email. Atlassian says changes take effect on your integration date, that the timeline is emailed, and that you also get a confirmation when the workspace is integrated. Do not use someone else's date.
  2. Sign in as an admin. Open Admin tools, then Billing, and read the Creator Lite count. Atlassian says that count is visible before integration, and that after integration those people are Creators.
  3. Open Admin tools, then Users. Choose Download Data, then Download last 6 months of user activity. Atlassian says to use that CSV to see who is actually using Loom.
  4. Decide who should stay a paid Creator. To remove someone before integration, Atlassian says: Admin tools, then Users, select the user, and follow the prompts to remove them from paid status.
  5. If you wait until after integration, Atlassian says to open Admin tools, then Workspace, then Manage settings and users in Atlassian Administration. Pick the organization, open Directory, then Users, then Actions, then Remove user.
  6. Plan the bill. Atlassian says there is no extra charge for the upgraded Creators until the next billing date. Anyone still active on that date is on the next invoice. Atlassian says it sends email reminders and in-app banners during the grace period. Atlassian also says you cannot keep the old price: pricing moves to current list prices, and the increase hits the first billing date after integration.
  7. Expect the product limits to change for people who are upgraded. Atlassian says they get unlimited recordings (no more 25-video limit), unlimited recording length (no more 5-minute limit), and the rest of the workspace features. Atlassian's recording-length page says Loom Starter users have a five-minute recording limit and a 25-video storage limit. Atlassian says recordings, the app, and settings stay, and that the integration does not remove access to content.
  8. After integration, billing is in Atlassian Administration. Atlassian says to open admin.atlassian.com/billing, pick the billing account if you have more than one, and find Loom under Products. If Loom is not there yet, Atlassian says the workspace has not been integrated.
  9. If login fails after integration, Atlassian says people who only had a Loom account should use the previous Loom email and password, and people who already had an Atlassian account should use those Atlassian credentials.

Atlassian says annual billing remains the most cost-effective option (up to ~17% savings) and points admins to current list prices. This page does not copy a dollar price, because that support article does not print one.

FAQ

Is there one public date when every Loom workspace loses Creator Lite?

No. Atlassian says Creator Lite users are upgraded on your integration date, and that your integration timeline is sent by email. Atlassian also says you get an email when the workspace has been integrated.

Will I be charged the day Creator Lite users become Creators?

Atlassian says no. Creator Lite users are upgraded to full Creator seats on the integration date, and you are not charged for those seats until your next billing date. Atlassian says any user you do not deactivate by that next billing date is included on the next invoice as a paid member.

What do upgraded Creator Lite users get?

Atlassian says upgraded users get unlimited recording capabilities (no more 25-video limit), unlimited recording length (no more 5-minute limit), and access to all full workspace features.

How do I see who would become a paid Creator?

Before integration, Atlassian says to sign in as an admin, open Admin tools, then Billing, and read the Creator Lite count. To see activity, open Admin tools, then Users, then Download Data, then Download last 6 months of user activity.

Do existing Loom recordings stay?

Atlassian says your Loom app, recordings, and settings stay the same, and that you will not lose access to content as part of the integration.
