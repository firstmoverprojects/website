EU Nov 1 Product ID: product identifiers become mandatory for low-value e-commerce imports

          
Next: Nov 1, 2026
The European Commission says product identifiers become mandatory on November 1, 2026 for goods sold in distance sales of imported goods with an intrinsic value not exceeding EUR 150. The guidance names a merchant product identifier, a manufacturer product identifier, and a standardised identifier such as an EAN where one exists. If a carrier or customs agent declares for you, ask them what product data they need.

          
  • Nov 1, 2026: Product identifiers become mandatory. The Commission's guidance says: "Effective enforcement starts from 1 November 2026."
  • Jul 1, 2028: The Commission lists the EU Customs Data Hub for e-commerce from this date.

          
Who is hit: Sellers outside the EU who ship online orders up to €150 to EU consumers, and the platforms, carriers and customs agents who declare those parcels.

          
Source: taxation-customs.ec.europa.eu · taxation-customs.ec.europa.eu · www.douane.nl. Last checked Oct 20, 2026.

IRS FIRE e-file system retires (1099s move to IRIS)

          
Next: Nov 1, 2026
The IRS is retiring FIRE (Filing Information Returns Electronically), the system many filers use to e-file Forms 1099 and other information returns. The IRS says existing applicants can update their applications through Nov. 9, 2026, after which they become read-only. The IRS says current FIRE users must complete a separate IRIS Application for TCC to file in IRIS. If a payroll provider or accountant files for you, this may already be handled. Ask them.

          
  • Nov 1, 2026: Last day to file test information returns through the FIRE test system.
  • Nov 9, 2026: Last day to make changes to IR Applications for TCCs.
  • Nov 19, 2026, 3 p.m. ET: Last day to file information returns through FIRE.
  • Jan 1, 2027: IRIS is the only electronic filing system for information returns, including prior-year returns and corrections.

          
Who is hit: Anyone who e-files Forms 1099 or other information returns through the IRS FIRE system themselves.

          
Source: www.irs.gov · www.irs.gov · www.irs.gov. Last checked Oct 12, 2026.

Form 941 for the third quarter of 2026

          
Next: Nov 2, 2026
The IRS fourth-quarter tax calendar and Publication 509 (2026) list November 2, 2026 to file Form 941 for the third quarter, and November 10, 2026 if the quarter's tax was deposited on time, properly, and in full. FUTA owed through September, if more than $500, is also due November 2.

          
  • Nov 2, 2026: File Form 941 for the third quarter. Deposit FUTA owed through September if more than $500.
  • Nov 10, 2026: File Form 941 for the third quarter only if deposits for the quarter were on time and in full.

          
Who is hit: Employers who file Form 941. Not sole proprietors with no employees, and not employers who file Form 944 instead.

Next: Dec 15, 2026
Publication 509 (2026) says corporations deposit the fourth installment of 2026 estimated income tax on December 15. The IRS calendar also lists Form 8813 for partnerships and the November employment-tax deposit for monthly depositors. Individual estimated tax is not due that day.

          
  • Dec 15, 2026: Corporate fourth estimated-tax installment, partnership Form 8813, and the monthly depositor's November employment-tax deposit.
  • Jan 15, 2027: Calendar-year individual estimated-tax installment under the Publication 509 timing rule. Not a December 15 payment.

          
Who is hit: Calendar-year corporations, partnerships that file Form 8813, and employers on the monthly deposit schedule.
