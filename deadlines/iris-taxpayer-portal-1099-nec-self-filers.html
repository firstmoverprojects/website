Feb 1, 2027, as we read it

How do you e-file your own 1099-NECs in the IRIS Taxpayer Portal for tax year 2026?

Apply for an IRIS Taxpayer Portal TCC first. Publication 5717 says you must use an IRIS Taxpayer Portal TCC in the portal, that a TCC obtained for FIRE cannot be used there, and to allow up to 45 calendar days for the application (sections 2.2.1 and 2.2.2).

The Instructions for Forms 1099-MISC and 1099-NEC say Form 1099-NEC is due on or before January 31, and that a date falling on a Saturday, Sunday, or legal holiday moves to the next business day. January 31, 2027 is a Sunday, so the due date is Feb 1, 2027 as we read it. The IRIS page says you must e-file if you have 10 or more information returns.

Who it affects

Small businesses that will type or upload their own Forms 1099-NEC for tax year 2026 in the IRIS Taxpayer Portal during the 2027 filing season. Publication 5717 says that beginning tax year 2026, IRIS will be the only intake system for information returns (section 1.1). It describes the portal as a no-cost way to prepare and file information returns without software or a third-party provider, by keying in the information or uploading a .csv file, up to 100 forms per submission (section 1.2), and it lists Form 1099-NEC among the forms the portal accepts (section 1.4).

Publication 5717 says any entity with an employer identification number (EIN) can apply for an IRIS Taxpayer Portal TCC (section 1.3). The application asks for the firm's EIN, and Publication 5717 says the system does not allow firms to use a Social Security Number (SSN) or Individual Taxpayer Identification Number (ITIN) in its place (section 2.2).

Who this does not apply to

This page is for businesses that file in the portal themselves. As we read the IRS pages below, it is not your checklist if someone else e-files your 1099-NECs:

  • A payroll provider or software that files for you. The IRIS page says that if you have third-party software or a service, you can use IRIS Application to Application (A2A), which starts with its own IRIS A2A TCC. Publication 5717 says A2A requires special software or a third-party provider (section 1.2), and that an IRIS A2A TCC cannot be used in the portal (section 2.2.2).
  • An accountant or other transmitter who files for you. Publication 5717 says a transmitter files for its own business and other businesses, can transmit for as many companies as it needs to under one TCC, and that the IRS encourages transmitters who file for multiple issuers to use one TCC for all of them (section 2.2).

If someone else files for you, ask them to confirm it before you also file. Publication 5717 says not to file identical information returns in more than one system, because that results in duplicate filing (section 2.2.2).

What to do

  1. Confirm that you, not a payroll provider, software, or an accountant, will file your 1099-NECs. If someone else files, the section above applies.
  2. Count all of your information returns. The IRIS page says that starting tax year 2023, if you have 10 or more information returns, you must file them electronically, and that this includes Forms W-2 e-filed with the Social Security Administration. The 1099-NEC instructions say the 10 is calculated by aggregating all information returns. Publication 5717 says corrected returns are not counted (section 1.2).
  3. Apply on the IRS IRIS Application for TCC page. Publication 5717 says a Responsible Official submits the application, and that it is processed after every Responsible Official signs with a 5-digit PIN and accepts the Terms of Agreement (sections 2.2 and 2.2.1). For the portal, it says to check the box next to Portal as the transmission method, and to choose the Issuer role if you file only for your own business (section 2.2).
  4. Allow up to 45 calendar days for processing. Publication 5717 says you can check the status of the application on your Application Summary page, and that information returns may not be transmitted through the portal until a TCC has been assigned (sections 2.2.1 and 2.2.2).
  5. Use the TCC that begins with D. Publication 5717 says the portal TCC is a five-character alphanumeric code that begins with the letter D, and that TCCs obtained for FIRE, AIR, IRIS A2A, or PBBA cannot be used in the portal (section 2.2.2).
  6. Check that each payment belongs on Form 1099-NEC. The 1099-NEC instructions say to enter nonemployee compensation of $2,000 or more in box 1a.
  7. File Form 1099-NEC with the IRS and furnish the recipient copies by Feb 1, 2027 as we read it. The instructions say you are required to furnish the payee statements and file with the IRS by January 31, and that a Saturday, Sunday, or legal holiday due date moves to the next business day. Publication 5717 says the portal lets you download recipient copies to distribute (sections 1.2 and 5).
  8. If you need more time, Publication 5717 says Form 1099-NEC is allowed only one nonautomatic 30-day extension, requested on a paper Form 8809 (section 4). If you need to correct a return you filed in the portal, the 1099-NEC instructions point to Publication 5717, which covers corrections in sections 3.6.7 and 3.6.8.
  9. If the application stays in Submitted Pending Review for more than 45 days, Publication 5717 says to contact the Help Desk (section 2.2.2). It lists 866-937-4130 (toll-free), Monday through Friday, 7:30 a.m. to 7:00 p.m. Eastern time (section 6.5).

What the IRS lists for returns due in 2027

The IRS information return penalties page says it charges separately for each information return not filed correctly or on time, and for each payee statement not provided correctly on time. It lists these amounts for returns and statements due in 2027:

The same page says the IRS may be able to remove or reduce a penalty if you acted in good faith and can show reasonable cause. It says you may have reasonable cause if you acted responsibly before and after the failure occurred, and you have significant reasons or the failure resulted from circumstances beyond your control. If you have reasonable cause and received Notice 972CG, the page says to respond within 45 days (60 days for a foreign filer) before the IRS assesses the penalty.

The page also lists ways to avoid a penalty: file and furnish before the due date, use correct information, file a waiver from electronic filing (Form 8508), or apply for an extension and file before the extended due date.

FAQ

Can I use my FIRE TCC in the IRIS Taxpayer Portal?

No. Publication 5717 says you must use an IRIS Taxpayer Portal TCC when transmitting information returns in the portal, and that TCCs obtained for FIRE cannot be used there (section 2.2.2). The portal TCC begins with the letter D.

How long does the IRIS TCC application take?

Publication 5717 says to allow up to 45 calendar days for application processing, and that the application is processed after every Responsible Official has signed with a PIN and accepted the Terms of Agreement (section 2.2.1). If it is in Submitted Pending Review for more than 45 days, it says to contact the Help Desk (section 2.2.2).

When is Form 1099-NEC for tax year 2026 due?

Feb 1, 2027 as we read it. The Instructions for Forms 1099-MISC and 1099-NEC say to file Form 1099-NEC on or before January 31, using either paper or electronic filing procedures, and to furnish payee statements by January 31. They say that if a date falls on a Saturday, Sunday, or legal holiday, the due date is the next business day. January 31, 2027 is a Sunday.

Do I have to e-file if I have fewer than 10 information returns?

The IRIS page says the e-file requirement applies if you have 10 or more information returns, including Forms W-2 e-filed with the Social Security Administration. The 1099-NEC instructions say that count adds up all information returns. As we read it, a business below 10 in total is not required to e-file, and the instructions give the same January 31 date for paper or electronic filing. The portal is open to any entity with an EIN that has an IRIS TCC (Publication 5717, section 1.3).

My payroll provider files my 1099-NECs. Do I need my own IRIS Taxpayer Portal TCC?

Not for that filing, as we read it. The IRIS page says software and third-party services use IRIS A2A with their own IRIS A2A TCC, and Publication 5717 says a transmitter can file for many companies under one TCC (section 2.2). Ask your provider to confirm they file your 1099-NECs, so the same returns are not filed twice (Publication 5717, section 2.2.2).
