Plain-English summary

ZenBusiness says its ZenBusiness Banking program is being discontinued. Deposits into ZenBusiness Banking accounts are disabled starting November 3, 2026, and ZenBusiness says incoming funds after that date will be rejected or returned to the sender. The accounts close on November 30, 2026, and debit cards linked to them stop working then. ZenBusiness says it will not move your money for you, and automatic payments and deposits do not move to a new account by themselves. For any balance over $1.00 left after closing, ZenBusiness says it mails a check to the address on file. ZenBusiness says statements and transaction records stay available on the Banking dashboard for at least 1 year, and suggests not starting transactions close to the deadline. You can move your funds to any bank you choose. Money Pro is a separate product and keeps working. Check ZenBusiness's own page for the current steps.

Copy-paste prompt for any AI agent

I run a small business. ZenBusiness's help page says ZenBusiness Banking deposits stop on November 3, 2026 and the accounts close on November 30, 2026. Please help me track this:
1. Make a checklist table of everything that pays money INTO my ZenBusiness Banking account (customers, payment apps, marketplaces, refunds, transfers), with a column for "new bank details sent yes/no".
2. Make a second table of everything that takes money OUT (payroll, taxes, loans, insurance, subscriptions, vendors, card-linked charges), with a column for "updated yes/no".
3. Remind me to check my mailing address on file and to download statements I want to keep.
4. Remind me to read ZenBusiness's closure page for the current steps, and list the questions to ask my bookkeeper.
5. Set up two reminders: October 30, 2026 (for the November 3 deposit cutoff) and November 20, 2026 (for the November 30 closing). (These reminder dates are my choice, not ZenBusiness's.)
Do not move money, open accounts or change settings. Do not ask me for passwords, account numbers or card numbers, and do not log in to any account. Only list what I should check and I will decide.
When you state any deadline or date, quote the exact line from the official source that states it. If you cannot find that line, say "no deadline line found" instead of guessing.
