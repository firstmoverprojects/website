Five short puzzle games you can play in your browser.

Shutter Cats

Every cat in the building wants to go home, each to its own window. Swipe a cat and it runs until something stops it. Flip the Day/Night switch to open sun or moon shutters, but every flip costs a move.

  • 24 puzzles, from one cat to four
  • Free Undo, and a Hint that shows the next best move
  • Touch, mouse or keyboard, in portrait or landscape
  • Free to play in your browser on itch.io. No ads, no accounts.

Chalkfair

A letter is chalked on the village slate. Fill four timber market stalls with words that match the category before the brass bell rings.

  • 96 campaign rounds, twelve at each of eight fairs
  • Daily Market (Daily Fair), Endless, Calm, and Speed
  • Tap a word chip, or type on a keyboard
  • Free to play in your browser on itch.io. No ads, no accounts.

Made with AI coding tools; see the itch.io page for the full AI disclosure.

Dippenmoor

Study the cloth swatch before the weaver's hood lowers, then mix the dye vats from memory until the linen matches. A wring shows how close you came.

  • 96 workshop orders, from simple dips to harder recipes
  • Daily Dye, Endless, Calm, and Hard
  • Tap a vat, or use the keyboard
  • Free to play in your browser on itch.io. No ads, no accounts.

Made with AI coding tools; see the itch.io page for the full AI disclosure.

Bunkhollow

Drag across the timber floorboards and divide each lodge floor into rooms. Every room holds one numbered sign, and the number matches that room's size.

  • 96 floors, twelve in each of eight lodges
  • Daily Lodge, Endless, and Calm
  • Each floor has one logical solution
  • Free to play in your browser on itch.io. No ads, no accounts.

Play Bunkhollow on itch.io

Made with AI coding tools; see the itch.io page for the full AI disclosure.

Quirebrook

Mend the blotted pages of a made-up village almanac. Solve the numbered clues and each letter fills the hidden saying, or type the saying and the letters fly back to the clues.

  • 36 seasonal pages across twelve months
  • Daily Page, a four-page Short Sitting, and Loose Pages
  • An ink pot of free hints that refills each day
  • Free to play in your browser on itch.io. No ads, no accounts.
