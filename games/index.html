Shutter Cats

Every cat in the building wants to go home, each to its own window. Swipe a cat and it runs until something stops it. Flip the Day/Night switch to open sun or moon shutters, but every flip costs a move.

  • 24 puzzles, from one cat to four
  • Free Undo, and a Hint that shows the next best move
  • Touch, mouse or keyboard, in portrait or landscape
  • Free to play in your browser on itch.io. No ads, no accounts.
