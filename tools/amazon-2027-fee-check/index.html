FAQ

When do the 2027 US FBA fee changes start?

Amazon's November 5, 2026 letter says most 2027 US referral and FBA fee changes take effect on January 15, 2027. On average, FBA fulfillment fees increase by $0.19 per unit sold, approximately 0.8% of the average item's selling price. Some products go up and some go down. The letter does not list a referral-fee percent.

What happens to stored inventory on February 1, 2027?

The same letter says that on February 1, 2027 Amazon drops the storage utilization surcharge and combines the aged inventory surcharge with monthly storage into one age-based rate. Fees are flat or lower for inventory that turns in under six months, and higher for aged inventory. The public letter does not list dollar rates or 180, 271, or 365 day prices.

What changes for bundles on January 11, 2027?

Amazon's seller notice says that on January 11, 2027 most bundles listed as one product must be packed by the original manufacturer or brand. Exceptions include a letter of authorization, gifting browse nodes, camera product types, and Virtual Bundles. Noncompliant listings are turned off after that date. This is a packing rule, not a fee rate.

Does the $19 kit calculate these fees?

No. The Amazon $1M Insurance Readiness Kit is a downloadable ZIP of checklists and templates for Amazon's $1 million commercial liability certificate. It does not calculate 2027 fees. The free check on this page is $0.

Is this an Amazon FBA fee calculator?

No. It is a free checker that shows which published 2027 changes apply to your setup. For your own SKU numbers, Amazon's letter points to the Fee Change Comparison dashboard in Profit Analytics and the Revenue Calculator.
