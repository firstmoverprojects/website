What this page uses

The SAM.gov entity registration page says: you must renew your registration every 365 days to keep it active. Updates can be made at any time, or during renewal.

The entity registration checklist (November 2024) says the same 365-day renewal, and says to allow at least ten business days after you submit the registration for it to become active in SAM.gov. This page counts ten weekdays after the submission date you enter. It skips Saturday and Sunday. It skips no federal holiday.

FAR 52.204-7 (NOV 2024) says an offeror is required to be registered in SAM when submitting an offer or quotation and at time of award.

FAR 52.204-13 says the contractor maintains registration in SAM during contract performance and through final payment of any contract, basic agreement, basic ordering agreement, or blanket purchasing agreement. To remain registered after the initial registration, the contractor reviews and updates the information on an annual basis, from the date of initial registration or subsequent updates. The SAM.gov page states the interval as 365 days. This page counts 365 days.

The GSA page on registering a business says you do not need to pay a third party to complete your registration.
