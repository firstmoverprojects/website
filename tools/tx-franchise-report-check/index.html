What this page uses

The Comptroller franchise tax page says the annual franchise tax report is due May 15. If May 15 falls on a weekend or holiday, the due date is the next business day. The same page's table for 2026 and 2027 lists a no-tax-due threshold of $2,650,000, a retail or wholesale rate of 0.375 percent, a rate of 0.75 percent for a business other than retail or wholesale, a compensation deduction limit of $480,000, an E-Z computation total revenue threshold of $20 million, and an E-Z rate of 0.331 percent.

The 2026 franchise tax forms page says the No Tax Due Report is not available for 2026 reports. It was discontinued for reports due on or after January 1, 2024. An entity with annualized total revenue at or below the threshold does not file that report, and still files a Public Information Report (Form 05-102) or an Ownership Information Report (Form 05-167).

The April 2026 Tax Policy News describes five paths that used to meet the old No Tax Due Report:

  • Annualized total revenue at or below the threshold: no franchise tax report, and still a Public Information Report or an Ownership Information Report.
  • A new veteran-owned business under Texas Tax Code Section 171.0005, for its initial five-year period: no franchise tax report, and no Public Information Report or Ownership Information Report during that period.
  • A passive entity under Texas Tax Code Section 171.0003: the long form or the E-Z Computation form, with the passive-entity circle darkened, and no Public Information Report or Ownership Information Report.
  • A real estate investment trust meeting Texas Tax Code Section 171.0002(c)(4): the long form or the E-Z Computation form, and a Public Information Report or an Ownership Information Report.
  • An entity above the threshold with zero Texas gross receipts: the long form or the E-Z Computation form, with zero on the Texas gross receipts line, and a Public Information Report or an Ownership Information Report.

The Public Information Report and Ownership Information Report page says Form 05-102 is for a corporation, limited liability company, limited partnership, professional association, or financial institution organized in Texas or with nexus in Texas. Form 05-167 is for another taxable entity in that same position. Each member of a combined group files its own report. The information report is due on the annual franchise tax report due date, including when no franchise tax report is required because revenue is at or below the threshold.
