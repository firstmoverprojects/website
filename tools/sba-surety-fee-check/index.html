What this page uses

Federal Register document 2026-22177, 91 FR 68580, published October 30, 2026, is a notice of Prior Approval Surety Bond Guarantee program fee changes. The authority lines in the notice are 15 U.S.C. 694b(h), 13 CFR 115.32(b) and (c), and 13 CFR 115.66.

The notice sets an applicability date of November 30, 2026. The fee change applies to SBA surety bond guarantees with an approval date on or after that day. The summary covers each guaranteed bond other than a bid bond, with a further exception for projects under NAICS Code 31, 32, 33, Manufacturing.

Under 13 CFR 115.32, for the Prior Approval program, the notice says the surety fee moves from 20 percent of the bond premium to 26 percent of the bond premium, and the contractor fee moves from $6.00 per thousand dollars of the contract amount to $7.29 per thousand dollars of the contract amount. For NAICS 31, 32, or 33, the surety fee remains 20 percent of the bond premium and the principal fee remains $6.00 per thousand dollars of the contract amount.

Under 13 CFR 115.66, for the Preferred Surety Bond program, the notice says the surety fee remains 20 percent of the bond premium and the principal fee remains $6.00 per thousand dollars of the contract amount.

The notice says the earlier rates, in place since October 1, 2018, were 20 percent of the bond premium and $6.00 per thousand dollars of the contract amount. This page uses those rates when the answers place the guarantee before November 30, 2026.

The dollar lines divide the premium by 100 and multiply by the percent, and multiply the contract amount by the per-thousand rate. A half cent rounds up. The notice states a rate per thousand dollars. It does not say to round the contract up to the next thousand.
