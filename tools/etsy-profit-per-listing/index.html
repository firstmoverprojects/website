Add the Sold Order Items CSV, and optionally one or more monthly statement CSVs. Profit is item revenue plus shipping charged, minus the fees on the statement for that order ID. Without a statement, the editable default rates below are used. Enter material cost and labor minutes per listing, or per variation.

Through January 4, 2027, the Share & Save holiday credit is 6.5% of the eligible order total. After January 4, 2027, that credit returns to 4%. Listing fees and payment processing still apply. A statement shows the credit that was posted.

What the numbers mean

  • Revenue is the item total plus the order's shipping, split across the items in that order by item total.
  • Fees matched from a statement are transaction, processing, listing or renewal, Offsite Ads, Share & Save credit, and regulatory operating fee. A credit lowers the fee total.
  • Margin is profit divided by revenue. A listing that loses money, or has a margin under 15%, is flagged.
  • Profit per labor hour uses the minutes you enter, times units, divided into profit.
