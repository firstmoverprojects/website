What this page uses

SBA SOP 50 10 8.1, "Lender and Development Company Loan Programs with Technical Policy Updates." The cover says "Published: September 25, 2026." It replaces SOP 50 10 8. The file named on the SBA SOP 50 10 page is "SOP 50 10 8.1 Technical Policy Updates effective 10.1.2026.docx".

Information Notice 5000-880695 (published August 14, 2026) says the SOP is "effective October 1, 2026, and will apply to all applications that are issued an SBA loan number on or after that date." Change-of-ownership rules are in Appendix 15. The table of contents starts that appendix on page 362.

Debt service coverage, Appendix 15, paragraph C.2.b

The Debt Service Coverage (DSC) ratio must be satisfied using either the last fiscal year-end or an average of the last two fiscal year-end statements on either a historical or adjusted basis based on the transaction type: Initial Acquisition: 1.25:1 / Business Expansion: 1.15:1 / Owner Buyout: 1.25:1 / ESOP and Cooperative: 1.25:1

The same paragraph says projections may satisfy coverage only for owner-occupied Special Purpose Property acquisitions where the appraisal fully collateralizes the loan. This page does not test that case.

Enter a partner buyout as an owner buyout if that is the category you will ask your lender to use. Owner buyout is the SOP category at 1.25:1.

Owner compensation "must be sufficient to support their current obligations and living expenses" when coverage is on an adjusted basis. This page does not calculate living expenses or owner pay.

Price above valuation, Appendix 15, paragraph C.1.b

The business valuation must support the Business Purchase Price ... regardless of how the debt is structured. If the amount paid for the business exceeds the business valuation, the difference must be made up by equity.

A quality of earnings report is required for business expansion and initial acquisition at a purchase price of $3 million or more. It must be "conducted for the benefit of the Lender" and "must not be prepared by or for the seller."

Limited equity sources, Appendix 15

Limited equity sources "may provide no more than half of the required Equity Injection." The cap is on all limited sources combined. Full-standby seller debt "may be considered as equity."

Additional Limited Equity Sources may be used when ... the sales price exceeds the value supported by the Business Valuation and Quality of Earnings report. In this situation, any additional funds provided must be on full standby.

Appendix 15 states an equity minimum of 10 percent for an initial acquisition, and that minimum cannot be reduced or eliminated. For expansion, buyout, and ESOP it is 10 percent but it can be reduced. This page does not convert 10 percent into dollars. Type the required equity injection you are asking your lender about.

Estimate

Dollars and cents. Zero is allowed except for debt service, which must be greater than zero. This page divides the numbers you type. It does not build a loan payment.

Which statements are you testing?

Paragraph C.2.b says the ratio uses either the last fiscal year-end or an average of the last two fiscal year-end statements, on a historical or adjusted basis. If you choose the average, this page takes an arithmetic mean and rounds a half cent up. It does not adjust the statements.

The denominator paired with that numerator. Greater than zero.

If this is above the valuation, paragraph C.1.b says the difference must be made up by equity.

Type the required equity injection you will ask the lender about. This page does not turn the 10 percent minimum into dollars.

Add every limited source together, including a full-standby seller note if you are counting it in that group. The cap is no more than half of the required equity injection.
