What this page uses

N.J.A.C. 12:11 is titled "ABC Test; Independent Contractors." It was proposed at 57 N.J.R. 894(a) and adopted May 5, 2026. The notice of adoption states an operative date of October 1, 2026. The NJDOL release dated May 5, 2026 gives an anticipated June 1, 2026 publication and effective date.

The NJDOL release says the rule sets out how the department applies the ABC test in N.J.S.A. 43:21-19(i)(6), and that the rule does not change that test. The release says the hiring business has the burden of proof and must meet all three prongs. The prongs are in section 12:11-1.3 (A), section 12:11-1.4 (B), and section 12:11-1.5 (C). Section 12:11-1.6(b) addresses Federal Form 1099.

The NJDOL release describes the laws this rule applies under: Unemployment Compensation, TDB, Wage and Hour, Wage Payment and Earned Sick Leave laws, and the Call Center Jobs Act.

  • Section 12:11-1.5(f): a business registration (sole proprietorship, LLC or corp) is "not alone sufficient to meet Prong C" (notice of adoption, page 172). An LLC alone does not prove contractor status.
  • Section 12:11-1.5(g): the worker's own liability or workers' comp insurance is "not alone sufficient" (notice of adoption, page 172). The contractor's own insurance alone does not prove contractor status.
  • A professional license alone is not sufficient (adoption discussion, pages 171 to 172).
  • Section 12:11-1.6(b): "One cannot transform an individual into an independent contractor ... by reporting the earnings ... using a Federal Form 1099" (notice of adoption, pages 176 to 177). A 1099 alone does not prove contractor status.
  • The rule lists the number of customers as a Prong C factor. This page does not score that number.
  • Proposed section 12:11-1.5(e), the free to work for others provision, was deleted on adoption (notice of adoption, page 205).

The questions ask whether each cited section has been reviewed, and whether a fact the notice says is not enough on its own is the only fact in use. This page does not restate other factors in the notice.
