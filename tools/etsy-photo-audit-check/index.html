Checks this page runs

  • Primary photo. Etsy Help says most listings must include at least one unedited photo of the actual item against a real background. On that photo you can crop, resize, add a watermark, or change the file format, but not adjust the lighting, and Etsy says not to use AI tools for those changes. Edits that make the item look different are not allowed on any photo. The unedited photo does not have to be first, but Etsy says the first photo should be the clearest, most realistic picture of what the buyer receives. Videos do not count toward the unedited photo. You mark whether the first photo is an unedited photo of the real item, or a mockup or render.
  • Exceptions. Etsy lists different photo rules for some items. Print-on-demand: a stock-photo mockup is allowed if the stock photo's owner has authorized it. Digital downloads: show a preview of the actual content. Customized items you make: show real finished items you made before. Patterns, recipes, and seeds: include at least one real, unedited photo of something made with your product. This page does not apply these exceptions for you, so read a mockup flag with them in mind.
  • Photo count. Enter a whole number from 1 to 10, the photos you want this audit to score. Etsy's listing help says a listing can hold up to 20 photos and 2 short videos. This page does not score photos 11–20. A count of 1 is a note, not a failed rule.
  • Variation photos. You mark whether each color or size option has a linked photo. This page does not claim Etsy requires that link. It records your answer. Etsy Help says one unedited photo per listing is enough, even if the listing has variations.
  • Disclosure. You mark whether a digital item or a print-on-demand production partner still needs a disclosure. Etsy's Handmade policy says a seller who uses a production partner must disclose the partner's name, location, and the work they do in the listing process. The name and partnership details can stay visible only to Etsy. The location and a description of the work appear to shoppers. Check the current policy for your item.
  • Title. Etsy's listing help says a title can be up to 140 characters, and says to avoid repetition. This page's repetition rule: a word of at least 3 letters or digits counts, except and, are, for, from, item, the, this, that, with, your. The same counted word 3 or more times is a repetition flag. The help page also says to consider fewer than 15 words. That 15-word line is a note, not a failed check. Character count runs after phone, email, SSN, and EIN redaction, with spaces at the ends removed. Redaction markers are not counted as title words.

    FAQ
    

    What does this free Etsy check do?
    

    Paste up to 10 Etsy listing rows, or toggle one listing's photos, variations, disclosure and title. Get a side-by-side risk list and real-photo fix plan. It runs in your browser. Nothing is uploaded. This page applies the checks written below to the text and toggles you enter. It cannot see your shop. It does not score a listing with a percent, and it does not estimate sales.
    

    What does Etsy say about the listing photo?
    

    Etsy Help says most listings must include at least one unedited photo of the actual item against a real background. You can crop or resize that photo, add a watermark, or change its file format, without AI tools. One unedited photo per listing is enough, even with variations. It does not have to be the first photo, but Etsy says the first photo should be the clearest, most realistic picture of what the buyer receives. Exceptions apply to print-on-demand, digital, customized, and made-to-order items. Etsy's listing help says a listing can hold up to 20 photos and 2 short videos. This page scores a whole number from 1 to 10 and does not score photos 11 through 20. A count of 1 is a note, not a failed rule.
    

    Did Etsy name a last day for this photo rule?
    

    No. The Etsy Seller Handbook article dated Oct 22, 2026 asks sellers to review listing images and update them as soon as possible. It says Etsy plans to begin proactively enforcing the image requirements in early 2027 and will give advance notice first. It does not name a date. If a buyer opens a case because the item is materially different from the listing, the seller may be responsible for the refund now.
