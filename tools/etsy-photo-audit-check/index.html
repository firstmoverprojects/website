Etsy Real-Photo & Listing Audit Check

Listed on The Runway (LaunchFree.io) — Upvote This Free Tool

Paste your listing, get a ranked fix plan. Free. Runs in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.

Who it's for: an Etsy seller checking a physical, digital, or print-on-demand listing before a buyer sees the photos and title.

This page applies the checks written below to the text and toggles you enter. It cannot see your shop. It does not score a listing with a percent, and it does not estimate sales.

Checks this page runs

  • Primary photo. Etsy Help says a listing must include at least one unedited photo of the actual item against a real background. Some exceptions may apply. Etsy also says the first photo should be the clearest realistic picture of what the buyer receives. That first-photo line does not say the unedited photo has to be first. You mark whether the first photo is an unedited photo of the real item, or a mockup or render.
  • Photo count. Enter a whole number from 1 to 10, the photos you want this audit to score. Etsy's listing help says a listing can hold up to 20 photos and 2 short videos. This page does not score photos 11–20. A count of 1 is a note, not a failed rule.
  • Variation photos. You mark whether each color or size option has a linked photo. This page does not claim Etsy requires that link. It records your answer.
  • Disclosure. You mark whether a digital item or a print-on-demand production partner still needs a disclosure. Etsy's Handmade policy says a seller who uses a production partner must disclose the partner's name, location, and the work they do. Some of that detail can be kept confidential to shoppers. Check the current policy for your item.
  • Title. Etsy's listing help says a title can be up to 140 characters, and says to avoid repetition. This page's repetition rule: a word of at least 3 letters or digits counts, except and, are, for, from, item, the, this, that, with, your. The same counted word 3 or more times is a repetition flag. The help page also says to consider fewer than 15 words. That 15-word line is a note, not a failed check. Character count runs after phone, email, SSN, and EIN redaction, with spaces at the ends removed. Redaction markers are not counted as title words.

1. Paste up to 10 listing rows

Five columns, in order: title, photo count, primary photo, variation photos, disclosure. Separate columns with a tab, a comma, or a semicolon. Put quotes around a title that contains the separator. A header row is optional.
