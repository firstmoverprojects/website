What this page uses

The Code of State Regulations file for 12 CSR 10-103, dated September 30, 2026, shows 12 CSR 10-103.185 section (1) in these words: every business, making sales of tangible personal property or rendering a taxable service, is required to file a combined sales/use tax return even though no (zero) (0) sales were made during the period covered by the return. The Department of Revenue posts the same text in its official-final-rules PDF.

The Department of Revenue sales tax FAQ says: every business with a sales tax license is required to file a return even though no sales were made during the period covered by the return.

A proposed amendment to 12 CSR 10-103.185 was filed June 15, 2026 and published in the Missouri Register, Volume 51, Number 14, July 15, 2026. The Department posts the same proposed text. The proposed sections read:

  • Section (1): every business that is registered for sales tax is required to file a sales tax return even though no sales were made during the period covered by the return.
  • Section (2): every business that is registered for consumer's use tax is required to file a consumer's use tax return even though no liability exists for the period covered by the return.
  • Section (3): every business that is registered for vendor's use tax is required to file a vendor's use tax return even though no Missouri destination sales were made for the period covered by the return.

The October 1, 2026 Missouri Register index lists 12 CSR 10-103.185 as proposed at 51 MoReg 879. The order column on that line is empty. The publication schedule in that issue lists December 30, 2026 as the Code effective date for an order of rulemaking published on November 2, 2026 or November 16, 2026.

12 CSR 10-104.030 sets the filing frequency from state sales tax, and the due dates. A monthly return is due on the last day of the following month. A quarterly return is due on the last day of the month following the quarter. An annual return is due on January 31 following the calendar year. A Saturday, Sunday, or Missouri state holiday moves that date to the next business day. State tax of $500 or more in a calendar month is filed monthly. Less than $500 in a calendar month, and $200 or more in a calendar quarter, is filed quarterly. Less than $200 in a quarter is filed annually. Local, conservation, and parks and soils taxes are left out of that frequency test.
