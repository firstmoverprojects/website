The rule this page uses

The November 2, 2026 Missouri Register has an order of rulemaking for 12 CSR 10-103.185, Filing Returns When No Liability Exists. The order adopts the text proposed on July 15, 2026. No changes were made, so the new text is not reprinted. The order says the amendment becomes effective 30 days after publication in the Code of State Regulations.

The October 1, 2026 Missouri Register schedule lists the dates for an order published on November 2, 2026. The Code publication date is November 30, 2026. The Code effective date is December 30, 2026.

The adopted text, from the July 15, 2026 Missouri Register, says:

  • A business registered for sales tax files a sales tax return even when no sales were made during the period.
  • A business registered for consumer's use tax files a consumer's use tax return even when no liability exists for the period.
  • A business registered for vendor's use tax files a vendor's use tax return even when no Missouri destination sales were made for the period.

The current Code of State Regulations and the Department's posted final rule still show the older sentence. That sentence says a business making sales of tangible personal property, or rendering a taxable service, files a combined sales and use tax return even when sales are zero. The Department's sales tax FAQ says every business with a sales tax license files a return even when no sales were made. Those are the rules in force before December 30, 2026.

The same FAQ sets the due date. A monthly return is due on the last day of the next month. A quarterly return is due on the last day of the month after the quarter. An annual return is due on January 31 of the next year. A Saturday, Sunday, or Missouri state holiday moves that date to the next business day.

Section 144.250, RSMo, adds 5 percent of the tax that should have been shown on a late return. It adds another 5 percent for each extra month, up to 25 percent. That addition does not apply when the failure is for reasonable cause and not willful neglect, evasion, or fraudulent intent. On a true $0 return, the percent applies to $0. If no return is filed, the director estimates the gross receipts and the tax. When gross sales tax is over $250 in one month, the statute adds no late amount for the first month.

To close an account, the Department's registration page says to file the final return and payments for every active account, then email businesstaxregister@dor.mo.gov or call 573-751-5860.
