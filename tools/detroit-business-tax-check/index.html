City of Detroit business tax check

Two questions. One result. Filing and payment are done with the Michigan Department of Treasury.

What changes on November 12, 2026

The business key-changes page says the Michigan Department of Treasury is upgrading the technology system for city income tax administration beginning November 12, 2026. On that date, Treasury puts City of Detroit business taxes into GenTax. City of Detroit taxpayers and third parties use Michigan Treasury eServices to file returns, make payments, and manage tax accounts online.

The Web Platform Impacts page says the City Tax eService and City Tax ePayments for City of Detroit income taxes were taken down on October 30, 2026 at 5:00 p.m. That work moves to Michigan Treasury eServices on November 12, 2026 at 7:00 a.m.

If this applies to you, do this on November 12, 2026 at 7:00 a.m.: open Michigan Treasury eServices and use it to file, pay, and manage the City of Detroit business tax account.

The same business page says:

  • An amended City of Detroit business tax return must be filed electronically. An original return can still be mailed.
  • Most payments can still be mailed with a check. City employer withholding tax must be paid electronically.
  • In-person and phone payments will not be accepted.
  • A checking or savings payment on the site has no fee. A debit card, credit card, or digital wallet payment has a fee.

The bulk-filing page is for Detroit employer withholding. Bulk filing is not available from October 30 through November 11, 2026. It resumes on November 12, 2026 at 7:00 a.m. The last period that could be bulk filed in Michigan Treasury Online was September 2026, due on or before October 15, 2026. The file format does not change. During the gap, the page says other electronic and paper submissions are held for processing, and a late return or payment may incur penalties and interest.

The business page does not say you must register the tax account again. For bulk withholding, it says the sign-in does not change: use your Michigan Treasury Online username and password. The bulk-filing page says the same thing if you already have a username and password for Michigan Treasury Online, Business Tax eService, Collections eService, or another MiLogin for Business service.

The taxes named on the business page are corporate income tax, employer withholding, partnership tax, and fiduciary income tax for estates and trusts. The parent page also links a separate individual income tax page and says those changes begin November 12, 2026. This check does not cover that individual return.
