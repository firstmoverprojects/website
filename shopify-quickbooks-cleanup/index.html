Shopify QuickBooks connector not working

These are the problems Shopify merchants describe most often in public reviews of the new Shopify Connector by QuickBooks—especially when settings, SKUs, or transaction types no longer match how you used to run the books.

  • Syncing stops. Orders sit in Integration transactions, nothing new posts, or sync runs every few hours and feels “stuck.” We trace connector settings, review queues, and overlap with prior imports—not Intuit-wide platform outages (see below).
  • Products don’t match. Line items arrive unlinked; SKUs or product names no longer map to QuickBooks items. We align Product Tracking settings and item lists where your scope allows.
  • Orders arrive as Sales Receipts instead of Invoices. Intuit’s current Shopify connector posts many standard (B2C) payments as Sales Receipts and B2B orders as Invoices. If your business always ran on Invoices, the books may no longer match your workflow. We help you reconcile within what the connector supports today—we cannot change Intuit’s product rules or fix their outages.
  • Tax codes flagged, not changed. We list blank, duplicate, or mismatched tax codes for you and your accountant. We don't change tax codes or sales tax settings without your accountant's written OK.
  • Catching up since the switch. Gaps, duplicates, or partial periods from the migration start date through today—without blindly re-importing on top of old data.

We cannot fix Intuit or Shopify outages. If QuickBooks or Shopify is down, sync is paused platform-wide, or Intuit’s servers are failing, we wait with you—we do not charge for work that is impossible until their systems recover.
